About Northbridge

Accounting that
feels personal.

We started Northbridge to give small business owners the kind of attention usually reserved for big clients.

Our story

Big-firm care for businesses that don’t have a finance team.

Too many small business owners only hear from their accountant once a year, usually with bad news. We wanted to do it differently: stay in touch all year, explain the numbers in plain English, and catch problems before they become expensive.

Today our small team works with contractors, shops, restaurants and service businesses across the Twin Cities.
